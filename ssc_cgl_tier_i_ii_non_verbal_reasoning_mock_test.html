50 Advanced Level MCQs (Pattern: Tier I & Tier II)

Section 1: Mirror & Water Images

Highly frequent in SSC CGL. Visualize the lateral inversion (mirror) or vertical inversion (water).

1. Find the exact MIRROR IMAGE of the given word when the mirror is placed on the right side:
E F F E C T I V E
  • A) E V I T C E F F E
  • B) Ǝ V I T Ɔ Ǝ ꟻ ꟻ Ǝ
  • C) Ǝ ꟻ ꟻ Ǝ Ɔ T I V Ǝ
  • D) E V I T C E ꟻ ꟻ Ǝ
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
The word is laterally inverted. The first letter in the mirror will be the inverted 'E' (Ǝ), followed by inverted V, I, T, inverted C (Ɔ), inverted E (Ǝ), inverted F (ꟻ), inverted F (ꟻ), and inverted E (Ǝ).
2. Find the exact WATER IMAGE of the given alphanumeric combination:
N U C L E A R 9 6
  • A) И U C ⅃ E ∀ ʁ 6 9
  • B) N ∩ C L E ∀ R 6 9
  • C) И ∩ C ⅃ E ∀ ʁ 9 9
  • D) И ∩ C ⅃ E ∀ ʁ 6 9
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: D
In a water image, top and bottom are inverted. N becomes И, U becomes ∩, C remains C, L becomes ⅃, E remains E, A becomes ∀, R becomes ʁ, 9 becomes 6, and 6 becomes 9.
3. Choose the correct MIRROR IMAGE (mirror placed on the right):
T A R G E T @ 2 4
  • A) 4 2 @ T Ǝ Ɔ Я A T
  • B) 4 2 @ T Ǝ ⅁ Я A T
  • C) 4 2 @ T E G R A T
  • D) 4 S @ T Ǝ ⅁ Я A T
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
The sequence reverses. '4' becomes horizontally flipped, '2' flips, '@' flips, followed by T, Ǝ, ⅁ (flipped G), Я (flipped R), A, T.
4. Find the WATER IMAGE of:
M A T H S 1 0 1
  • A) W ∀ T H S 1 0 1
  • B) W ∀ T H Ƨ I 0 I
  • C) W ∀ ⊥ H Ƨ 1 0 1
  • D) M ∀ ⊥ H S I 0 I
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
Water image inverts vertically: M -> W, A -> ∀, T -> ⊥, H -> H, S -> Ƨ, 1 -> 1 (with downward hook), 0 -> 0, 1 -> 1.
5. Find the MIRROR IMAGE of a clock showing 3:15.
  • A) 8:45
  • B) 9:15
  • C) 8:45
  • D) 9:45
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: A
Formula for Mirror Image of Clock: 11:60 - Given Time. 11:60 - 3:15 = 8:45. (Visually, hour hand is slightly past 3, which reflects to slightly before 9. Minute hand at 3 reflects to 9).
6. Find the WATER IMAGE of a clock showing 8:20.
  • A) 10:10
  • B) 9:10
  • C) 10:40
  • D) 9:40
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: A
Formula for Water Image of Clock: 18:30 - Given Time. 18:30 - 8:20 = 10:10.
7. Find the correct MIRROR IMAGE of the figure sequence:
◁ ◯ ▷ ◆
  • A) ◆ ◁ ◯ ▷
  • B) ◆ ◁ ◯ ◁
  • C) ◆ ◁ ◯ ▷
  • D) ◆ ◁ ◯ ◁
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
The sequence reverses to ◆, then ▷ mirrors to ◁, ◯ remains ◯, and ◁ mirrors to ▷.
8. Which of the following numbers looks the same in a mirror?
  • A) 818
  • B) 10101
  • C) 8008
  • D) All of the above
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
8008 laterally inverted remains 8008.
9. Choose the WATER IMAGE of:
G R 9 8 A
  • A) ⅁ ʁ 6 8 ∀
  • B) ⅁ ʁ 9 8 ∀
  • C) G ʁ 6 8 ∀
  • D) ⅁ R 6 8 A
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: A
Vertical flip: G becomes ⅁, R becomes ʁ, 9 becomes 6, 8 remains 8, A becomes ∀.
10. Find the MIRROR IMAGE (mirror at right) of:
B R I S K
  • A) ꓘ S I R B
  • B) ꓘ Ƨ I Я ꓭ
  • C) ꓘ Ƨ I R ꓭ
  • D) K S I R B
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
Reverse order and invert letters horizontally: K -> ꓘ, S -> Ƨ, I -> I, R -> Я, B -> ꓭ.

Section 2: Counting of Figures

Apply standard SSC CGL counting tricks (n(n+1)/2, symmetric grids, internal diagonals).

11. How many triangles are there in a square that is divided by both of its diagonals?
  • A) 4
  • B) 6
  • C) 8
  • D) 10
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
Trick: In a square divided by diagonals, count the small triangles inside (which are 4) and multiply by 2. (4 x 2 = 8).
12. How many triangles are present in a regular pentagram (a 5-pointed star drawn with continuous lines)?
  • A) 5
  • B) 8
  • C) 10
  • D) 12
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
A 5-pointed star always contains exactly 10 triangles (5 small external triangles + 5 large internal triangles formed by the intersecting lines).
13. Find the number of squares in an 8x8 standard chessboard.
  • A) 64
  • B) 144
  • C) 204
  • D) 256
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
Formula for total squares in an n x n grid is sum of squares: 1² + 2² + 3² + ... + n². For n=8: 1+4+9+16+25+36+49+64 = 204.
14. How many rectangles (including squares) are there in an 8x8 chessboard?
  • A) 204
  • B) 1296
  • C) 64
  • D) 144
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
Formula for rectangles in an m x n grid is (m(m+1)/2) * (n(n+1)/2). Here m=8, n=8. (8*9/2) * (8*9/2) = 36 * 36 = 1296.
15. How many triangles are in a triangle which is divided into 4 smaller bases by 3 vertical lines drawn from the top vertex to the base?
  • A) 4
  • B) 8
  • C) 10
  • D) 12
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
Trick: Number the base segments (1, 2, 3, 4). Add them up: 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10 triangles.
16. A triangle has its 3 medians drawn, intersecting at the centroid. How many total triangles are formed?
  • A) 12
  • B) 16
  • C) 18
  • D) 20
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
A triangle with all 3 medians drawn yields exactly 16 triangles (6 small, 3 made of two small, 6 made of three small, and 1 large main triangle).
17. A rectangle is divided into 4 rows and 3 columns. How many total squares are there?
  • A) 12
  • B) 18
  • C) 20
  • D) 24
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
Trick for m x n grid: (m x n) + ((m-1) x (n-1)) + ((m-2) x (n-2))... until 0. Here 4x3: (4x3) + (3x2) + (2x1) = 12 + 6 + 2 = 20.
18. Two distinct squares share exactly one common vertex. What is the maximum number of straight lines required to draw this figure?
  • A) 6
  • B) 7
  • C) 8
  • D) 9
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
A single square requires 4 distinct lines. If they only share a vertex (a point), no lines are shared. Total lines = 4 + 4 = 8.
19. How many straight lines are required to construct a regular hexagon that has all of its diagonals drawn?
  • A) 12
  • B) 15
  • C) 18
  • D) 21
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
Lines = Edges + Diagonals. Edges = 6. Diagonals in an n-sided polygon = n(n-3)/2 = 6(3)/2 = 9. Total lines = 6 + 9 = 15.
20. A square contains a smaller square connecting the midpoints of its sides. How many total triangles are in this figure?
  • A) 4
  • B) 8
  • C) 12
  • D) 16
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: A
Connecting midpoints of a square forms a diamond (inner square). The space between the inner and outer square consists of exactly 4 triangles at the corners.

Section 3: Cubes & Dice

Test of spatial visualization. Opposite faces and open dice folding are staple Tier I questions.

21. Two positions of a dice are shown. In position 1, visible faces are {1, 3, 5}. In position 2, visible faces are {2, 3, 6}. Which number is opposite to 3?
  • A) 1
  • B) 4
  • C) 5
  • D) 6
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
Rule of 1 common number: Write adjacent faces in clockwise order starting from the common number (3). Dice 1: 3-5-1. Dice 2: 3-6-2. Therefore, 5 is opposite 6, 1 is opposite 2. The remaining number 4 must be opposite 3.
22. A cube is painted red on all faces and then cut into 27 smaller equal cubes. How many smaller cubes have exactly one face painted red?
  • A) 4
  • B) 6
  • C) 8
  • D) 12
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
Formula for 1 face painted: 6(n-2)², where n is number of cuts along an edge. Total cubes = n³ = 27, so n=3. 6(3-2)² = 6(1)² = 6. (These are the center cubes of each of the 6 faces).
23. From the same 27-cube setup above, how many cubes have NO faces painted?
  • A) 1
  • B) 3
  • C) 4
  • D) 8
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: A
Formula for 0 faces painted (inner core): (n-2)³. For n=3, (3-2)³ = 1³ = 1.
24. An open dice is given as a cross shape. The vertical stem has 1, 2, 3, 4. To the left of 2 is 5, to the right of 2 is 6. Which number is opposite to 2?
  • A) 1
  • B) 3
  • C) 4
  • D) Cannot be determined
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
In an open dice straight line, alternate blocks are opposite. Stem is 1-2-3-4. 1 is opposite 3. 2 is opposite 4. The side wings (5 and 6) are opposite each other. Thus, 4 is opposite 2.
25. Two positions of a dice show {A, B, C} and {C, D, E}. What letter is opposite to C? (Assuming letters are A to F).
  • A) A
  • B) B
  • C) D
  • D) F
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: D
C is adjacent to A, B, D, and E. The only remaining face that C can be opposite to is F.
26. In a standard dice, what is the sum of opposite faces?
  • A) 6
  • B) 7
  • C) 8
  • D) 9
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
By definition, a "standard dice" always has opposite faces summing to 7 (1+6, 2+5, 3+4).
27. If faces {2, 4, 6} share a common corner, can this be a standard dice?
  • A) Yes
  • B) No
  • C) Cannot be determined
  • D) Depends on the other numbers
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: A
In a standard dice, 2 is opposite 5, 4 is opposite 3, 6 is opposite 1. Because 2, 4, and 6 do not contain any opposite pairs among themselves, they can mutually share a single corner. Yes, it can be.
28. An open dice has symbols @, #, $, %, &, *. If @ is alternate to $, # is alternate to &, what is opposite to %?
  • A) @
  • B) $
  • C) *
  • D) #
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
In open dice folds, alternate surfaces are opposite. Since @ is opposite $, and # is opposite &, the remaining pair % and * must be opposite each other.
29. Three positions of a dice: 1) {2,3,4} 2) {3,4,5} 3) {4,5,6}. What is opposite to 4?
  • A) 1
  • B) 2
  • C) 3
  • D) 6
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: A
Look at the faces adjacent to 4: 2, 3 (from pos 1), 3, 5 (from pos 2), 5, 6 (from pos 3). So 4 is adjacent to 2, 3, 5, 6. The only remaining face is 1. Therefore, 1 is opposite 4.
30. A cube is painted Blue on two opposite faces, Red on two opposite faces, and Green on two opposite faces. It is cut into 64 small cubes. How many small cubes have exactly 3 faces painted?
  • A) 4
  • B) 8
  • C) 16
  • D) 24
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
Regardless of the number of cuts (n=4 here since 4³=64), the number of cubes with exactly 3 faces painted is always the 8 corner cubes (unless the cube is cut differently or unpainted, which it isn't).

Section 4: Paper Folding & Cutting

Visualize the symmetry. A cut made on folded paper mirrors across the fold axes when opened.

31. A square transparent paper has a diagonal line drawn from top-left to bottom-right. If the paper is folded exactly in half horizontally (bottom folded up to top), what does the folded paper look like?
  • A) A square with an 'X'
  • B) A square with a 'V' shape
  • C) A rectangle with a 'V' shape
  • D) A rectangle with a single diagonal line
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
The original square is folded into a rectangle (half height). The bottom half of the diagonal line overlaps the top half, reflecting across the horizontal fold, forming a 'V' shape.
32. A circular paper is folded in half, then folded into a quarter. A small circular hole is punched exactly in the center of this quarter. How many holes will appear when unfolded?
  • A) 1
  • B) 2
  • C) 4
  • D) 8
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
The paper was folded into 4 overlapping layers. A single punch goes through all 4 layers, resulting in 4 symmetrically placed holes upon unfolding.
33. A square paper is folded diagonally to form a triangle. The top sharp corner is cut off parallel to the base. What is the shape of the paper when unfolded?
  • A) A square with the center missing
  • B) An octagon
  • C) A square with 4 corners cut off
  • D) A square with the center cut out as a square
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: D
When folded diagonally, the center of the square becomes the mid-point of the longest edge (hypotenuse). Cutting the top tip of this folded triangle actually cuts a piece out of the exact center of the original unfolded square.
34. A square sheet is folded vertically in half, then horizontally in half to form a small square. A diagonal cut is made from the center of the original sheet to the corner of the small square. When opened, what shape is the central hole?
  • A) Circle
  • B) Square / Diamond
  • C) Cross
  • D) No central hole
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
The corner of the small square that corresponds to the center of the unfolded sheet is cut diagonally. When unfolded 4 ways, this forms a 4-sided figure rotated 45 degrees, which is a diamond/square.
35. If a translucent paper with a pattern of an arrow pointing UP (↑) is folded exactly down the middle vertically, what is the result?
  • A) The arrow disappears
  • B) You see a single arrow pointing UP (↑)
  • C) You see an arrow pointing UP with a thicker stem
  • D) You see half of an UP arrow vertically sliced
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: D
If the arrow is perfectly centered and symmetrical, folding the paper exactly down the vertical centerline will superimpose the left half of the arrow onto the right half, showing a vertically bisected arrow.
36. A paper is folded twice to form a square, and a single triangle is punched near the outer edge. Unfolding will reveal how many triangles?
  • A) 2
  • B) 4
  • C) 8
  • D) 1
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
Two folds means 4 layers of paper. A punch creates 4 identical shapes, symmetrically arranged near the edges.
37. A hexagonal paper is folded in half. A semi-circle is cut out of the folded edge. When unfolded, what is seen in the center?
  • A) Two semi-circles
  • B) A single full circle
  • C) An oval
  • D) A square
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
The fold acts as an axis of symmetry. A semi-circle cut on the fold will mirror itself when opened, forming a complete perfect circle.
38. A square paper is folded diagonally twice to form a smaller triangle. Two small circles are punched in the center of the triangle. Unfolding reveals how many circles?
  • A) 4
  • B) 6
  • C) 8
  • D) 12
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
Folding diagonally twice creates 4 layers. Two holes punched through 4 layers = 2 x 4 = 8 circles.
39. A rectangular paper is folded in half lengthwise. A right-angled triangle is cut out from the open edge (not the folded edge). When unfolded, what is the shape of the cut on the sides?
  • A) A single diamond in the middle
  • B) Two identical right-angled triangles on opposite edges
  • C) Two triangles forming a larger triangle on one edge
  • D) Four triangles on all corners
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
Since the cut is on the open edge (the boundaries), unfolding merely mirrors the cut to the other extreme edge. It does not combine them.
40. A square sheet is folded along its diagonal. A line is drawn perpendicular to the fold. If cut along this line, what shapes do you get?
  • A) Three triangles
  • B) Two squares
  • C) Two smaller triangles and a diamond
  • D) One large triangle, two small triangles
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: A
When a folded triangle is cut parallel to its base, you get the small top triangle (which unfolds into a diamond) and the bottom piece (which unfolds into two triangles). Total pieces: 3.

Section 5: Figure Matrices & Series Completion

Tier II level logic. Track movement of multiple elements, alternating rules, and mathematical logic on shapes.

41. Solve the 3x3 Pattern Matrix:
◯
◐
●
□
◧
■
△
?
▲
  • A) △
  • B) ◭
  • C) ◮
  • D) Both B and C are valid
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: D
Row rule: Empty Shape -> Half-filled Shape -> Fully-filled Shape. The middle shape must be a half-filled triangle (either left-filled ◧ style or right-filled).
42. Figure Analogy: If (Square → Octagon) then (Triangle → ?)
  • A) Hexagon
  • B) Pentagon
  • C) Heptagon
  • D) Circle
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: A
Logic: Number of sides is multiplied by 2. Square (4) -> Octagon (8). Triangle (3) -> Hexagon (6).
43. Complete the visual series: ⭡, ⭥, ⭣, ⭥, ?
  • A) ⭥
  • B) ⭡
  • C) ⭣
  • D) ⭤
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
The sequence is an alternating pendulum: Up, Up/Down, Down, Up/Down, Up...
44. Figure Series: A line segment rotates clockwise by 45° in each step. If it starts pointing North (↑), what direction does it point in the 6th step?
  • A) South (↓)
  • B) South-West (↙)
  • C) West (←)
  • D) North-West (↖)
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
Step 1: N (0°). Step 2: NE (45°). Step 3: E (90°). Step 4: SE (135°). Step 5: S (180°). Step 6: SW (225°).
45. Matrix Rule: Row 1 has 1 dot, 2 dots, 3 dots. Row 2 has 2 dots, 4 dots, 6 dots. Row 3 has 3 dots, 6 dots, ? dots.
  • A) 7
  • B) 8
  • C) 9
  • D) 12
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
Multiplication table logic: Column 1 × Row number. Row 3: 3x1=3, 3x2=6, 3x3=9.
46. Which shape is the odd one out?
△, □, ⬠, ◯
  • A) △
  • B) □
  • C) ⬠
  • D) ◯
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: D
A circle has no straight lines or corners; the rest are polygons (Triangle, Square, Pentagon) made of straight line segments.
47. Find the missing shape based on addition logic: ◒ + ◓ = ●. Therefore, ◧ + ? = ■
  • A) ◨
  • B) ◩
  • C) ◪
  • D) □
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: A
Lower half filled + Upper half filled = Fully filled circle. Left half filled (◧) + Right half filled (◨) = Fully filled square (■).
48. Analogy: ⚀ is to ⚅ as ⚂ is to ?
  • A) ⚁
  • B) ⚃
  • C) ⚄
  • D) ⚅
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
Relationship is opposite faces on a standard dice (sum to 7). 1 is opposite 6. Therefore, 3 is opposite 4.
49. A clock's minute hand is at 12 and hour hand is at 9. What is the angle between them? Then, the minute hand moves 15 minutes. What is the new angle?
  • A) 90°, 180°
  • B) 90°, 172.5°
  • C) 270°, 180°
  • D) 90°, 165°
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: B
At 9:00, angle is 90°. At 9:15, minute hand is at 3 (90° from 12). Hour hand moved for 15 mins (15 * 0.5° = 7.5° past 9). Angle = 180° - 7.5° = 172.5°.
50. Grouping of identical figures: You have 9 figures. (1,4,7) are triangles. (2,5,8) are squares. (3,6,9) are circles. Figures 1,2,3 contain a dot. Figures 4,5,6 contain a line. Figures 7,8,9 contain a cross. Which set represents a valid logical grouping?
  • A) (1,2,3), (4,5,6), (7,8,9)
  • B) (1,4,7), (2,5,8), (3,6,9)
  • C) Both A and B are valid groupings
  • D) None of the above
Show Answer & Logic
Answer: C
In SSC CGL figure grouping, elements can be grouped by shape (B) OR by internal element (A). Both are logically sound and mutually exhaustive groups.
