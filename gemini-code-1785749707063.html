Practice Exam: 50 Multiple Choice Questions

History

1. Who founded the Maurya Empire?
  • Ashoka
  • Chandragupta Maurya
  • Bindusara
  • Harsha
Answer: B) Chandragupta Maurya
2. The Battle of Buxar was fought in which year?
  • 1757
  • 1761
  • 1764
  • 1765
Answer: C) 1764
3. Where was the first session of the Indian National Congress held?
  • Calcutta
  • Madras
  • Bombay
  • Delhi
Answer: C) Bombay
4. Who was the first Governor-General of independent India?
  • C. Rajagopalachari
  • Lord Mountbatten
  • Rajendra Prasad
  • Lord Dalhousie
Answer: B) Lord Mountbatten
5. The Indus Valley Civilization was famous primarily for:
  • Town Planning
  • Agriculture
  • Trade
  • Military strength
Answer: A) Town Planning
6. Who built the Sanchi Stupa?
  • Gautama Buddha
  • Chandragupta
  • Ashoka
  • Kanishka
Answer: C) Ashoka
7. Which movement was launched concurrently with the Khilafat Movement?
  • Civil Disobedience Movement
  • Non-Cooperation Movement
  • Quit India Movement
  • Swadeshi Movement
Answer: B) Non-Cooperation Movement
8. Who is the author of 'Arthashastra'?
  • Megasthenes
  • Kalidasa
  • Chanakya (Kautilya)
  • Banabhatta
Answer: C) Chanakya (Kautilya)
9. The silver coin 'Rupaya' was first issued by:
  • Akbar
  • Sher Shah Suri
  • Alauddin Khalji
  • Babur
Answer: B) Sher Shah Suri
10. In which year did the Quit India Movement start?
  • 1930
  • 1940
  • 1942
  • 1947
Answer: C) 1942
